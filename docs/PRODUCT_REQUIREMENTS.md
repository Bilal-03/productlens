# Product Requirements

ProductLens serves product managers, analysts, growth practitioners, founders, and revenue teams. It must answer governed KPI, trend, comparison, ranking, segmentation, funnel, retention, cohort, revenue, feature-adoption, acquisition, and diagnostic questions.

The flagship question is “Why did checkout conversion fall last week?” A successful response quantifies the change, affected segments, contribution, evidence, a clearly marked hypothesis, recommended investigation, business action, and follow-up questions.

Non-goals are data modification, generated Python, arbitrary SQL, real-time streaming, enterprise multi-tenancy, complex agents, and ungoverned causal claims.
